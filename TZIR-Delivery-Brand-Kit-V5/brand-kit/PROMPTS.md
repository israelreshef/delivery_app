# Image generation record

The two atmospheric master backgrounds were generated with the built-in image generation tool. All logos, typography, icons, platform crops and final compositions were created deterministically in the project.

## Vertical master prompt

Use case: ads-marketing

Asset type: master vertical brand atmosphere background for WhatsApp and Instagram Story

Primary request: create a premium abstract urban courier-route background for an Israeli legal and business delivery company

Scene/backdrop: top-down night city grid inspired by central Tel Aviv and Gush Dan, abstract rather than a literal map, with one elegant luminous route moving through a few precise nodes

Style/medium: sophisticated high-end editorial 3D graphic, clean corporate design, subtle depth, premium technology-and-trust mood

Composition/framing: vertical 9:16, generous calm negative space in the upper-middle and center for later deterministic logo and copy placement, details concentrated toward the lower third and outer edges

Lighting/mood: deep navy night, restrained electric blue route glow, small ice-white highlights, confident and professional, not futuristic sci-fi

Color palette: #07162c deep navy, #145ddb blue, #5aa0ff light blue, #e8f1ff ice white

Text: no text

Constraints: no logo, no letters, no numbers, no people, no vehicles, no flags, no recognizable private addresses, no watermark; keep the center uncluttered; crisp enough for a 1440 by 2560 mobile background

Avoid: neon overload, cyberpunk, generic stock gradients, busy map labels, cartoon style, paper texture, red or orange accents

## Wide master prompt

Use case: ads-marketing

Asset type: master wide brand atmosphere background for social covers and campaign headers

Primary request: create a premium abstract urban courier-route background for an Israeli legal and business delivery company

Composition/framing: wide 16:9 landscape, calm negative space across the left and central third for later deterministic logo and copy placement, route and city detail concentrated on the right and lower edge

Lighting/mood: deep navy night, restrained electric blue route glow, small ice-white highlights, confident and professional, not futuristic sci-fi

Color palette: #07162c deep navy, #145ddb blue, #5aa0ff light blue, #e8f1ff ice white

Text: no text

Constraints: no logo, no letters, no numbers, no people, no vehicles, no flags, no recognizable private addresses, no watermark; crisp enough for a 1920 by 1080 cover

Avoid: neon overload, cyberpunk, generic stock gradients, busy map labels, cartoon style, paper texture, red or orange accents
